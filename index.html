Internal Medicine Fellowship Decision Instrument

Rheumatology vs. Hematology / Oncology

An attending-perspective diagnostic assessment covering clinical reasoning, bad-day tolerance, emotional load, prestige bias, and long-term career architecture.

Most residents choose a fellowship based on the most exciting 10% of a rotation. Long-term attending satisfaction is decided by the routine 70% and the hardest 20%.

This 36-item instrument (~7 minutes) is built to surface tensions you may not have put side by side yet:

1. Ambiguity vs. ProofGray-zone clinical synthesis vs. biopsy & molecular certainty
2. Bad-Day ToleranceChronic pain & paperwork vs. relapse & futile-care pressure
3. Emotional Carrying CostQuality-of-life restoration vs. mortality & goals-of-care
4. Iatrogenic Risk & AcuityOutpatient cadence vs. neutropenia, TLS & acute toxicity
5. Clinical CraftMSK exam, US & joints vs. smears, marrow & staging
6. Prestige vs. Daily JoyDetects when status pull diverges from Tuesday energy
7. Academic & GCC RealityUndersupplied private clinic vs. tertiary cancer center
8. Head vs. Life AlignmentFlags if intellectual pull & life sustainability disagree
How to answer: Go with your honest gut reaction — not what an attending would applaud. Progress saves automatically on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.
